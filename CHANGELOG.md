# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

- Added the pinyin tone letters and the Latin letters of DIN 91379 (including drawn Ƈ ƈ Ɨ ɨ Ǥ ǥ Ʒ ʒ), six combining marks, the modifier letters ʹ ʺ ʾ ʿ ˈ ˌ, eight arrows (← ↑ → ↓ ↖ ↗ ↘ ↙, drawn by Wayne Shih for Public Sans), the Bitcoin sign ₿ and a drawn capital sharp s ẞ (U+1E9E) in every weight and in the italic, and the superscript and subscript signs ⁺ ⁻ ⁼ ⁽ ⁾ and ₊ ₋ ₌ ₍ ₎ (U+207A – U+207E, U+208A – U+208E).
- Changed existing text: marks stacked on a letter now land where the precomposed letter puts them, also in the italic fonts, where the accent of 38 letters such as `ž ź š ś Ȓ Â` sat up to 169 units off its letter, the bar of `ŧ` is lighter in Bold and Black, the em dash has the en dash's side bearings and no longer uses `calt`, capital `A` is kerned against `m n r p h b` and similar letters, the trademark and service mark signs sit at cap height, the strokes of `$` and `¢` are lighter in Regular, Bold and Black, and the ring of `å Å ů Ů ǻ Ǻ` is thicker with an open hole while italic diaeresis dots are spaced like the upright, the Black `5` has a clean join where the diagonal meets the bowl, the slash of `%` and `‰` is thicker in Regular and Black, `~` and `≈` are stronger in Black, the Black tilde and macron accents are lighter, the tilde wave is more distinct and the hook above a breve is lower, the caron of `ď ľ ť Ľ` is a straight wedge and `ď` and `ľ` are wider and kerned so it keeps at least 40 units from a following letter, bracket, quote or `T V W Y`, the italic superior figures `⁰` – `⁹` are shifted along the slant like the subscripts, the italic subscripts `₂` and `₇` are moved back onto the slant in Regular to Black, and the diaeresis dots are lower at Thin and Regular and larger at Regular, with the round dots and stacked marks following, and a mark typed after an accented letter now lands above its accent also on the `cv03`, `cv04`, `cv05` and `ss03` forms and after the diaeresis letters, because the stored `top` anchor of 37 to 139 glyphs per master now matches its mark, and eight alternates (`Ǐ Ḯ ǎ ȧ ǟ` with `cv04` or `cv05`, three of them also with `ss03`) have the width of their alternate letter instead of the plain one, so they no longer run into the next letter.
- Added new optional forms: the `onum` feature now draws true oldstyle figures (`0 1 2` at x-height, `3 4 5 7 9` hanging below the baseline, `6 8` at full height) in every weight and in the italic; Public Sans maps `onum` to the lining figures; the opt-in letter alternates `cv01` (wider `J`), `cv03` (round `G`), `cv04` (single-storey `a`) and `cv05` (`I` with serifs), with every accented form, the pinyin and DIN 91379 letters included, and the Ĳ forms that follow `cv01` and `cv05`; and the opt-in digit forms `zero` (slashed `0`) and `cv06` (`1` with a base), which also work with `onum` and `tnum`; and the opt-in stylistic set `ss03`, which replaces the square dots of `i`, `j`, the dot accents, the diaeresis, punctuation and the comma shapes with round ones in 180 glyphs, among them every dotted letter of the pinyin and DIN 91379 sets and the dotted `cv03`, `cv04` and `cv05` alternates.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-10

- Added Offentlig Sans, a fork of Public Sans (upstream commit `e40fcf567`) renamed to Offentlig Sans.
- Fixed four Public Sans issues: italic `å` rendered as `ǻ`, subscript digits U+2080 – U+2089 were unencoded, a spaced en dash lost its spaces in every weight except Black, and capital `Ǔ` (U+01D3) was missing.
- Split `ss01` into `ss01` (`g`) and `ss02` (`l`), and made Regular (400) the default of the variable fonts.
