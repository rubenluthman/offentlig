# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

- Changed kerning of `T V W Y` before `ȉ`: the gap is now 40 – 67 units at every weight, upright and italic (Regular was 247, Black 323). `Pî`, `Yĩ` and `Yǐ` tighten by 1 – 11 units in the same groups. No pair gets looser.
- Changed the dots to round by default: the period, comma, colon, `i j`, the diaeresis and the other dotted characters use the circular dots that were under `ss03`, the dot shape of IBM Plex, which Offentlig Serif and Mono will adapt, so the family shares one dot. `ss03` now gives the square dots of Public Sans. Widths are unchanged. Kerning moves with the dots: the round `i` kerns as it did under `ss03` (Thin `Ti` 0 to -100, Regular -66; `Vi` Regular -14, Black -40), `ĳ` follows it, and the exceptions of `ŀ` before `ﬁ ﬂ` and of `ị̈Ħ ị̂H` in Black Italic are fitted again.
- Changed `W` and `N` in Bold, ExtraBold and Black, upright and italic: the notches between their diagonals are cut deeper, so the lower vertices of `W` and the crotches of `N` hold less ink and no longer fill in as far as in other fonts. The widest circle at the `W` vertices falls from 1.31 – 1.34 to 1.04 – 1.10 times the stem, and at the `N` crotches from 1.43 – 1.48 to 1.32 – 1.36. Advance widths and spacing are unchanged, and Regular and lighter weights are unchanged.
- Changed the ring of `Å` and `Ů`: it is now a round ring of its own, set lower and a little smaller, so `Å` no longer reaches into the descenders of the line above at the default line spacing (the Black ring was 45 units into them). The ring keeps 56 units from the apex of `A` in Black. `å` and `ů` are unchanged.
- Changed the ogonek on `ą ę į ų ǫ ǭ` and the capitals: it is about 85 units deeper (lowest point -405, -415 and -425 at Thin, Regular and Black), its tip is 20 units shorter, and on `ę` it hangs under the end of the arm instead of under the bowl. Advances are unchanged; 22 pairs per master gain kerning, mostly `ę` before `g j ḻ ṟ ƒ`.

## [0.6.0] - 2026-11-19

- Added case-sensitive punctuation: the hyphen, dashes, `( ) [ ] { }`, `@` and the colon rise to the capital centre in `case`. The hyphen, dashes and colon also rise through `calt` between capitals and figures (`2026-11-18`, `12:30`, `ABC-123`), and `@` before a capital; next to lowercase they stay where they were (`E-post`). Parentheses, square brackets and braces rise in `case` only, so a pair never comes out with one bracket raised and the other low.
- Changed kerning in the heavy weights: `T` and `Y` swap through `calt` to a copy with a shorter right arm before letters with a tall mark, so `Tö` kerns like `To`, and `Yö`, `Yë` and `Yō` at most 66 units looser than `Yo` (before, `Tö` was 38 and `Yö` 130 units looser in Black). The `Y` arm ends 100 units lower along its own edges, so its angle and stroke stay those of the plain `Y`. Regular and lighter weights are unchanged.
- Changed the narrow double grave on `ȉ ȑ` from Medium to Black: it is up to 37 units narrower on each side, so in Black it no longer overhangs further than the breve on `ĭ` (100 and 114 units, was 137 and 151). Typed `ị̏ į̏ ɨ̏ ṛ̏ ṟ̏ ŗ̏` now take the narrow mark too. Pairs with `ȉ ȑ` use group kerning with the same values.

## [0.5.1] - 2026-11-18

- Added four space characters: the figure space (U+2007, 1400 units, the tabular figure width), the punctuation space (U+2008, as wide as the period at each weight), the thin space (U+2009, 333 units) and the narrow no-break space (U+202F, 333 units, a sixth of an em), for grouping digits in Swedish and French numbers and aligning tables. The figure, thin and narrow no-break spaces have the same advance at every weight, the punctuation space follows the period, and none of the four kerns. Nothing else changed.

## [0.5.0] - 2026-11-18

- Added tabular oldstyle figures: with `tnum` and `onum` both on, in either order, figures are oldstyle on the shared 1400-unit width (they were lining), and `zero` and `cv06` carry through. The oldstyle `1` keeps its base. Nothing else changed.
- Changed the double grave on `ȉ ȑ` and on typed `i ı r` followed by U+030F: a narrow mark, centred on the letter, replaces the full-width one. It no longer overhangs the letter by up to 390 units in Black, so `Tȉ Vȉ Yȉ`, `fȉ`, `ȉl`, brackets, quotes and `cv05` `Iȉ` keep at least 30 units of clearance in every weight. `ȁ ȅ ȍ ȕ` and the capitals are unchanged.
- Fixed spacing: a grave, macron, tilde, breve or caron typed after `T V W Y` on a letter with no precomposed form (the lowercase DIN 91379 sequences, Yoruba `ẹ̀ ọ̄ ṣ̀`, Igbo `ụ̀`, Lithuanian `ą̀ į̃`) no longer runs into the capital, at every weight. Hidden copies of the capitals, swapped in by `rclt`, carry the extra kerning.

## [0.4.0] - 2026-11-17

- Fixed the dotless `ı` (and the Turkish `i` under `locl`): its advance now equals `i` in every weight, so Turkish, Azerbaijani, Kazakh, Tatar and Crimean Tatar text no longer sets `i` narrower. Every accented `i` built on it is wider by the same amount (`í ì î ǐ ĭ į ȉ ỉ`, the typed `ị ɨ` with marks, and `ï ĩ ī` in Thin and the italics): +17 units in Thin, +16 in Thin Italic, about +11 in Regular, −1 in Black, so they now equal `i` in all 18 statics.
- Fixed kerning: serifed `I` (`cv05`) stays within 60 units of plain `I` against `v f t r` and round letters (it was up to 96 units looser in Regular and 125 in Black); the diaeresis floor of 8 units now applies only to `ï ḯ ị̈ ɨ̈` next to `H l i b k h d` in ExtraBold and Black Italic, and 14 pairs that were tighter than in Public Sans (such as Regular Italic `ïT`, `fï`) keep at least its gap; every pair of two characters keeps at least 15 units between diagonal outlines (`ốV`, `î₹`, `ťĪ`, `(ĥ`, `ïb` and others); `đ` kerns like `d`.
- Changed drawings: the round dots of `ss03` have the same area as the square dots, upright and italic; the lower right wall of `G` with `cv03` is thicker in Bold and Black; the stress marks `ˈ ˌ` are longer (440 instead of 330 units).

## [0.3.0] - 2026-11-16

- Added the prime marks ′ ″ ‴ (U+2032 – 2034) and the superscript modifier letters ʰ ʳ ˢ ᵈ ᵗ ⁿ of DIN 91379. The `sups` feature sets typed h, r, s, d, t and n as these letters after a superior figure, so `1st 2nd 3rd 4th` becomes ¹ˢᵗ ²ⁿᵈ ³ʳᵈ ⁴ᵗʰ, while `1er`, `Ste` and `century` stay as typed. The optional letters now also change the accented and barred letters built on them (`ss01` `ĝ ğ ģ`, `ss02` `ľ ł`, `cv03` `Ǥ`, `cv05` `Ɨ`), so a word never mixes two designs.
- Fixed spacing inherited from Public Sans, worst in Black: accented letters no longer run into a capital's arm (`Yö`, `Tè`), a quote or bracket (`"în`, `(sí)`), an `f` (`fî`), another accent (`ĴÏ`) or the next letter (`îl`, `Ời`), also when typed as a letter plus combining marks and with `cv04` and `ss03` on. Every pair with an accented letter now keeps at least 30 units, or the gap of the same pair without accents where that is smaller, and every pair of two plain characters keeps at least 30 units (`Ax`, `ƒ ₣ ¥ Æ Ł` next to their neighbours, and the doubled daggers `†† †‡ ‡† ‡‡` in Thin). The diaeresis pairs `ïï ïĩ ïī īï ïị̈ ïɨ̈ īị̈ īɨ̈` are kerned apart at every weight, upright and italic (they collided in the italic from ExtraLight up), and `Yğ Yǧ` with `ss01` kern in the italic as they do upright. The same holds with the alternate forms on, each alone and in the combinations that meet in one pair (`ss01 ss02 ss03 cv01 cv03 cv04 cv05 cv06 zero onum`, `salt`, and several together: `ŀt`, `ĺȈ`, `Ợȁ`, `7·` and `î1` no longer collide). `ŀ` with `ss02` is 28 – 49 units wider so the dot clears the next letter like the default `ŀ`, and `ỉ` is 15 units narrower in Black Italic, matching `ı`. `ǥ` is spaced like `g` with `ss01`, with a longer bar in Thin. Pairs whose outlines touched (`ḯĦ Ħḯ ǐ² ǰ²`, and `ïĦ Ħï` in italic) now keep 30 units or more, and the pairs `ŀt ŀﬁ ŀﬂ ŀf` and `ḯĦ ǐ² ĭ²` in Black are no longer 190 – 400 units loose. Common pairs such as `To` and `Ye` are unchanged. The added kerning makes the fonts larger.
- Fixed marks and letters: combining accents typed after `V v Q q ð þ Þ ŋ ĸ Ə ƒ ẞ ɨ ị` sit on the letter, `į ị ɨ` lose their dot under a top accent, a mark below `j ı ȷ` sits under the letter, and the round-dot (`ss03`) Turkish `i` has the dot size of `i`. Marks typed below `J W f g w x y Q` now sit under the letter, `ȓ` has its breve where typed `r` + U+0311 puts it, the italic o-horn and u-horn letters with a stacked mark take a further mark on the accent, and U+03A9 shapes to the same `Ω` as U+2126. The bars of `₩` and `₦` are lighter and sit further apart, so no counter is a pinhole. Also fixed the dotless `ı` in Bold to Black Italic, which was darker and wider than `i`, the bar of `Ǥ` in the heavy weights, and `sups` after a raised plus, minus or equals (`x(n+t)`).

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-13

- Added the 18 pinyin tone letters and 108 precomposed Latin letters of DIN 91379 (Latin Extended-A, Extended-B and Extended Additional; Greek, Cyrillic and the superscript modifier letters are not covered), seven combining marks (U+030D, U+0310, U+0315, U+0325, U+032E, U+0331, U+035F), drawn Ƈ ƈ Ɨ ɨ Ǥ ǥ Ʒ ʒ, the modifier letters ʹ ʺ ʾ ʿ ˈ ˌ, eight arrows (← ↑ → ↓ ↖ ↗ ↘ ↙, drawn by Wayne Shih for Public Sans), the Bitcoin sign ₿, a drawn capital sharp s ẞ (U+1E9E), and the superscript and subscript signs ⁺ ⁻ ⁼ ⁽ ⁾ ₊ ₋ ₌ ₍ ₎. Marks typed after a letter now land where the precomposed letter puts them, also in the italic.
- Added optional forms: true oldstyle figures under `onum`, the letter alternates `cv01` (wider `J`), `cv03` (round `G`), `cv04` (single-storey `a`) and `cv05` (`I` with serifs) with their accented letters, the digit forms `zero` (slashed `0`) and `cv06` (`1` with a base), and `ss03`, which replaces the square dots of the dotted glyphs with round ones. `ss02` and `salt` also swap `fl` and the Catalan `ŀ`, so one word never shows both forms of `l`. The default letters and figures are unchanged.
- Changed existing text: the em dash uses the en dash's side bearings and no `calt`; `A` is kerned before `m n r p h b` and similar letters; `™` and `℠` sit at cap height; `$` and `¢` have lighter strokes; the ring of `å Å ů Ů ǻ Ǻ` is thicker; the diaeresis sits lower at Thin and Regular (by 54 and 34 units of 2000 per em, with the Public Sans dot size) and its italic dots are spaced like the upright; accents on 38 italic letters sit on their anchors; the Black `5`, the `%` and `‰` slash, the Black `~ ≈`, the tilde, the macron and the hook above a breve were redrawn (the italic hook keeps clear of the breve); the variable fonts no longer carry unused helper glyphs; the caron of `ď ľ ť Ľ` is a straight wedge that keeps clear of the next letter; the italic superior figures follow the slant; `locl` applies to every language it lists, including Romanian; and `ẞ`, `Ĳ ĳ ŉ`, `G` with `cv03` and the oldstyle `7` have kerning groups of their own.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-10

- Added Offentlig Sans, a fork of Public Sans (upstream commit `e40fcf567`) renamed to Offentlig Sans.
- Fixed four Public Sans issues: italic `å` rendered as `ǻ`, subscript digits U+2080 – U+2089 were unencoded, a spaced en dash lost its spaces in every weight except Black, and capital `Ǔ` (U+01D3) was missing.
- Split `ss01` into `ss01` (`g`) and `ss02` (`l`), and made Regular (400) the default of the variable fonts.
