# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

- Fixed `sups` after a raised plus, minus or equals: `x(n+t)`, `x(n−t)` and `x(n=t)` now set `ⁿ` and `ᵗ` like `x(n)`. A typed hyphen-minus stays as it is.
- Fixed the Turkish `i` with round dots (`ss03`): its dot now has the size of the dot of `i` in every weight (it was up to 12 percent smaller).
- Fixed `į` with a top accent: it loses its dot like `i`, `ị` and `ɨ` (`į́`); a typed U+0307 still brings the dot back.
- Fixed a mark below `j`, `ı` and `ȷ`: `j̣ ı̣ ȷ̣` now put the dot under the letter instead of on top of it.
- Fixed kerning around `i` with a mark in the heavy weights: the typed `ị` and `ɨ` with a breve, diaeresis or caron now kern after `T V Y f`, quotes and brackets like `ĭ ï ǐ` (`Vị̈` was −145 against `Vï` 39); the round dots (`ss03`) of `ï ḯ ị̈ ɨ̈` no longer close up before `H l i b k h d` in Black Italic (`ïi`, `ɨ̈i`); and `ĭ` clears `l i b k h H` in Black and Black Italic (`ĭl` was −15, inherited from Public Sans), as does the typed `ị̆ ɨ̆`. Plain pairs are unchanged.
- Fixed the round dots (`ss03`) closing up where the default dots do not, in the heavy weights and above all in Black Italic: `fï ḟï` (−75 against 10), `Ơi Ới Ời` and the other `Ơ Ư` forms before `i`, `ïT ïW ïV ïY` and their accented letters, `"ï 'ï ’ï ”ï /ï ₣ï Fï Zï 7ï`, `ïħ ï\ ï7 ïŉ ï1`, `!ǐ ?ḯ !ḯ !ǰ` and `ṯj ḻj` in Light. Every pair with a round-dot glyph now keeps at least the gap of its default pair, up to 30 units (2 units less at most), at all 18 weights. Plain pairs and every pair with `ss03` off are unchanged.
- Fixed typed `ị` and `ɨ` with an acute, grave, macron, tilde, circumflex, breve, diaeresis or caron running into the next letter from Bold up (Igbo `ị́ ị̀` before `H` or `l`): they now space like `í ì ī ĩ î ĭ ï ǐ`.
- Added the prime marks ′ ″ ‴ (U+2032 – 2034), drawn like the modifier letters ʹ ʺ, and the superscript modifier letters ʰ ʳ ˢ ᵈ ᵗ ⁿ (U+02B0, U+02B3, U+02E2, U+1D48, U+1D57, U+207F) of DIN 91379. The letters stand on the baseline of the superior figures and have close side bearings. The `sups` feature sets typed h, r, s, d, t and n as these letters only after a superior figure, a superior parenthesis or another of these letters, so `1st 2nd 3rd 4th` becomes ¹ˢᵗ ²ⁿᵈ ³ʳᵈ ⁴ᵗʰ and `x(n)` becomes `x` followed by ⁽ⁿ⁾, while `1er`, `Ste` and `century` stay as typed.
- Fixed combining accents typed after `V v Q q ð þ Þ ŋ ĸ Ə ƒ ɨ ẞ ị`: they now sit on the letter instead of beside it, and `ị` and `ɨ` lose their dot under a top mark (`ị́ ị̀`).
- Fixed accented lowercase letters after `T V W Y` and their accented capitals: the accent no longer touches the arm (`Tȉ`, `Yö`, `Yë`, `Tè`, `Yŏ` and many more, worst in Black), also with the single-storey `a` (`cv04`) and the round dots (`ss03`) on. Plain pairs such as `To`, `Ye` and `Wa` are unchanged.
- Fixed thin mark tips that still came close to a neighbour after `T V W Y` and next to `ǐ ǰ ő ï` and brackets (`Tǐ`, `Yǐ`, `Yē`, `Tĩ`, `Tȋ`), and the heavy-weight collisions inherited from Public Sans: `îl îb îh îk îi` (Romanian), and `Ơ Ư` with their accented forms before the lowercase that follows, such as `Ời`, `Ơi`, `Ườ` and `Ưi` (Vietnamese). Plain pairs are unchanged.
- Fixed brackets, quotes and marks touching accented letters, inherited from Public Sans and worst in Black: closing punctuation after `í ï ĩ ī ĭ ǐ ȉ ȋ ḯ`, the typed `ị` and `ɨ` with an accent, and `ŕ ř ȓ ĵ ű ő ơ ỵ ĝ ğ ǧ ć` and more (`(sí)`, `(betű)`, `(pepř)`, `thơ?`), and opening punctuation before `ì ĭ ȍ ȅ ĵ ḻ` and more (`(ìhè)`, `¿ĵ?`, `[ĭ`), with the round dots (`ss03`) on as well. Only `ȉ` and `ȑ` stay close to opening punctuation until their narrower double grave is drawn. Plain pairs are unchanged.
- Fixed the bar of `Ǥ` in the heavier weights, most in Black: it no longer starts against the lower curve with a hairline gap, and now floats clear of the curve and the crossbar on the left of the stem.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-13

- Added the 18 pinyin tone letters and 108 precomposed Latin letters of DIN 91379 (Latin Extended-A, Extended-B and Extended Additional; Greek, Cyrillic and the superscript modifier letters are not covered), seven combining marks (U+030D, U+0310, U+0315, U+0325, U+032E, U+0331, U+035F), drawn Ƈ ƈ Ɨ ɨ Ǥ ǥ Ʒ ʒ, the modifier letters ʹ ʺ ʾ ʿ ˈ ˌ, eight arrows (← ↑ → ↓ ↖ ↗ ↘ ↙, drawn by Wayne Shih for Public Sans), the Bitcoin sign ₿, a drawn capital sharp s ẞ (U+1E9E), and the superscript and subscript signs ⁺ ⁻ ⁼ ⁽ ⁾ ₊ ₋ ₌ ₍ ₎. Marks typed after a letter now land where the precomposed letter puts them, also in the italic.
- Added optional forms: true oldstyle figures under `onum`, the letter alternates `cv01` (wider `J`), `cv03` (round `G`), `cv04` (single-storey `a`) and `cv05` (`I` with serifs) with their accented letters, the digit forms `zero` (slashed `0`) and `cv06` (`1` with a base), and `ss03`, which replaces the square dots of 181 glyphs with round ones. `ss02` and `salt` also swap `fl` and the Catalan `ŀ`, so one word never shows both forms of `l`. The default letters and figures are unchanged.
- Changed existing text: the em dash uses the en dash's side bearings and no `calt`; `A` is kerned before `m n r p h b` and similar letters; `™` and `℠` sit at cap height; `$` and `¢` have lighter strokes; the ring of `å Å ů Ů ǻ Ǻ` is thicker; the diaeresis sits lower at Thin and Regular (by 54 and 34 units of 2000 per em, with the Public Sans dot size) and its italic dots are spaced like the upright; accents on 38 italic letters sit on their anchors; the Black `5`, the `%` and `‰` slash, the Black `~ ≈`, the tilde, the macron and the hook above a breve were redrawn (the italic hook keeps clear of the breve); the variable fonts no longer carry unused helper glyphs; the caron of `ď ľ ť Ľ` is a straight wedge that keeps clear of the next letter; the italic superior figures follow the slant; `locl` applies to every language it lists, including Romanian; and `ẞ`, `Ĳ ĳ ŉ`, `G` with `cv03` and the oldstyle `7` have kerning groups of their own.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-10

- Added Offentlig Sans, a fork of Public Sans (upstream commit `e40fcf567`) renamed to Offentlig Sans.
- Fixed four Public Sans issues: italic `å` rendered as `ǻ`, subscript digits U+2080 – U+2089 were unencoded, a spaced en dash lost its spaces in every weight except Black, and capital `Ǔ` (U+01D3) was missing.
- Split `ss01` into `ss01` (`g`) and `ss02` (`l`), and made Regular (400) the default of the variable fonts.
