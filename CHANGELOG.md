# Changelog

## [0.3.0] - 2026-11-16

- Added the prime marks ′ ″ ‴ (U+2032 – 2034) and the superscript modifier letters ʰ ʳ ˢ ᵈ ᵗ ⁿ of DIN 91379. The `sups` feature sets typed h, r, s, d, t and n as these letters after a superior figure, so `1st 2nd 3rd 4th` becomes ¹ˢᵗ ²ⁿᵈ ³ʳᵈ ⁴ᵗʰ, while `1er`, `Ste` and `century` stay as typed. The optional letters now also change the accented and barred letters built on them (`ss01` `ĝ ğ ģ`, `ss02` `ľ ł`, `cv03` `Ǥ`, `cv05` `Ɨ`), so a word never mixes two designs.
- Fixed spacing inherited from Public Sans, worst in Black: accented letters no longer run into a capital's arm (`Yö`, `Tè`), a quote or bracket (`"în`, `(sí)`), an `f` (`fî`), another accent (`ĴÏ`) or the next letter (`îl`, `Ời`), also when typed as a letter plus combining marks and with `cv04` and `ss03` on. Every pair with an accented letter now keeps at least 30 units, or the gap of the same pair without accents where that is smaller, and every pair of two plain characters keeps at least 30 units (`Ax`, `ƒ ₣ ¥ Æ Ł` next to their neighbours, and the doubled daggers `†† †‡ ‡† ‡‡` in Thin). The diaeresis pairs `ïï ïĩ ïī īï ïị̈ ïɨ̈ īị̈ īɨ̈` are kerned apart at every weight, upright and italic (they collided in the italic from ExtraLight up), and `Yğ Yǧ` with `ss01` kern in the italic as they do upright. The same holds with the alternate forms on, each alone and in the combinations that meet in one pair (`ss01 ss02 ss03 cv01 cv03 cv04 cv05 cv06 zero onum`, `salt`, and several together: `ŀt`, `ĺȈ`, `Ợȁ`, `7·` and `î1` no longer collide). `ŀ` with `ss02` is 28 – 49 units wider so the dot clears the next letter like the default `ŀ`, and `ỉ` is 15 units narrower in Black Italic, matching `ı`. `ǥ` is spaced like `g` with `ss01`, with a longer bar in Thin. Pairs whose outlines touched (`ḯĦ Ħḯ ǐ² ǰ²`, and `ïĦ Ħï` in italic) now keep 30 units or more, and the pairs `ŀt ŀﬁ ŀﬂ ŀf` and `ḯĦ ǐ² ĭ²` in Black are no longer 190 – 400 units loose. Common pairs such as `To` and `Ye` are unchanged. The added kerning makes the fonts larger.
- Fixed marks and letters: combining accents typed after `V v Q q ð þ Þ ŋ ĸ Ə ƒ ẞ ɨ ị` sit on the letter, `į ị ɨ` lose their dot under a top accent, a mark below `j ı ȷ` sits under the letter, and the round-dot (`ss03`) Turkish `i` has the dot size of `i`. Marks typed below `J W f g w x y Q` now sit under the letter, `ȓ` has its breve where typed `r` + U+0311 puts it, the italic o-horn and u-horn letters with a stacked mark take a further mark on the accent, and U+03A9 shapes to the same `Ω` as U+2126. The bars of `₩` and `₦` are lighter and sit further apart, so no counter is a pinhole. Also fixed the dotless `ı` in Bold to Black Italic, which was darker and wider than `i`, the bar of `Ǥ` in the heavy weights, and `sups` after a raised plus, minus or equals (`x(n+t)`).

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-13

- Added the 18 pinyin tone letters and 108 precomposed Latin letters of DIN 91379 (Latin Extended-A, Extended-B and Extended Additional; Greek, Cyrillic and the superscript modifier letters are not covered), seven combining marks (U+030D, U+0310, U+0315, U+0325, U+032E, U+0331, U+035F), drawn Ƈ ƈ Ɨ ɨ Ǥ ǥ Ʒ ʒ, the modifier letters ʹ ʺ ʾ ʿ ˈ ˌ, eight arrows (← ↑ → ↓ ↖ ↗ ↘ ↙, drawn by Wayne Shih for Public Sans), the Bitcoin sign ₿, a drawn capital sharp s ẞ (U+1E9E), and the superscript and subscript signs ⁺ ⁻ ⁼ ⁽ ⁾ ₊ ₋ ₌ ₍ ₎. Marks typed after a letter now land where the precomposed letter puts them, also in the italic.
- Added optional forms: true oldstyle figures under `onum`, the letter alternates `cv01` (wider `J`), `cv03` (round `G`), `cv04` (single-storey `a`) and `cv05` (`I` with serifs) with their accented letters, the digit forms `zero` (slashed `0`) and `cv06` (`1` with a base), and `ss03`, which replaces the square dots of the dotted glyphs with round ones. `ss02` and `salt` also swap `fl` and the Catalan `ŀ`, so one word never shows both forms of `l`. The default letters and figures are unchanged.
- Changed existing text: the em dash uses the en dash's side bearings and no `calt`; `A` is kerned before `m n r p h b` and similar letters; `™` and `℠` sit at cap height; `$` and `¢` have lighter strokes; the ring of `å Å ů Ů ǻ Ǻ` is thicker; the diaeresis sits lower at Thin and Regular (by 54 and 34 units of 2000 per em, with the Public Sans dot size) and its italic dots are spaced like the upright; accents on 38 italic letters sit on their anchors; the Black `5`, the `%` and `‰` slash, the Black `~ ≈`, the tilde, the macron and the hook above a breve were redrawn (the italic hook keeps clear of the breve); the variable fonts no longer carry unused helper glyphs; the caron of `ď ľ ť Ľ` is a straight wedge that keeps clear of the next letter; the italic superior figures follow the slant; `locl` applies to every language it lists, including Romanian; and `ẞ`, `Ĳ ĳ ŉ`, `G` with `cv03` and the oldstyle `7` have kerning groups of their own.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-10

- Added Offentlig Sans, a fork of Public Sans (upstream commit `e40fcf567`) renamed to Offentlig Sans.
- Fixed four Public Sans issues: italic `å` rendered as `ǻ`, subscript digits U+2080 – U+2089 were unencoded, a spaced en dash lost its spaces in every weight except Black, and capital `Ǔ` (U+01D3) was missing.
- Split `ss01` into `ss01` (`g`) and `ss02` (`l`), and made Regular (400) the default of the variable fonts.
