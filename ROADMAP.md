### Offentlig Sans
<!-- vale HouseStyle.Headings = YES -->

- [x] Pinyin tone letters and the Latin letters of DIN 91379, see public-sans issues #258 and #277.
- [x] Swedish diacritics: a larger ring with an open centre, diaeresis position and dot size.
- [x] A drawn capital sharp s `ẞ` (U+1E9E), see public-sans PR #194 and issue #144.
- [x] Optional letter forms: wide `J`, round `G`, single-storey `a`, serifed `I`, `1` with a base, slashed zero, round dots.
- [x] Oldstyle figures, and the items of Rosalie Wagner's design to-do list (public-sans issue #158) that were drawn: italic accent alignment, `™ ℠` position, tilde, macron, hook above a breve, `%`, `≈`, the Black `5`, italic superior figures and the caron of `ď ľ ť Ľ`.
- [ ] The rest of that list: italic glyphs that were sheared without corrections, the terminals of `? J j Ŋ ŋ` and the shape of the ogonek (the issue gives no target). Curve tension was reviewed and changes no glyph visibly, so it is left as in Public Sans.
- [x] Em dash spacing, trademark alignment, the Bitcoin sign, arrows and A and m spacing, see public-sans issues #151, #204, #215, #216, #259 and PR #252.
- [x] Superscript and subscript `+ − = ( )`, and the DIN 91379 modifier letters `ʹ ʺ ʾ ʿ ˈ ˌ`.
- [ ] Prime marks `′ ″ ‴` and the DIN 91379 superscript modifier letters `ʰ ʳ ˢ ᵈ ᵗ ⁿ`.
- [ ] Kern accented lowercase letters after T V W Y so the accent clears the arm (inherited from Public Sans; Yö, Yë, Tè and the double-grave letters collide in Black).
