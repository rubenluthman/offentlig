# UFO and designspace as the source format
Status: Active · 2026-11-06

## Decision

`sources/*.designspace` and `sources/*.ufo` are the only source of truth for outlines, kerning, groups and OpenType features. Do not reintroduce `.glyphs` files or build from them.

- The upstream `.glyphs` files were patched with the three upstream fixes, renamed, and converted once with glyphsLib 6.17.2 (`--minimal`). They are not kept in the repository.
- The Regular master (`wght` 400) was first generated by interpolating Thin and Black, and is a regular master from now on. It is no longer derived from Thin and Black, so a change to Thin or Black does not reach Regular unless it is repeated there.
- Upstream fixes that are not yet merged upstream stay in these sources and are tracked in `ROADMAP.md`. Upstream changes are merged by hand as UFO edits.
- Vertical metrics live in the UFO `fontinfo.plist` files and are recorded in `ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## Why

Offentlig Sans starts from Public Sans `develop` at commit `e40fcf567` (https://github.com/uswds/public-sans). Upstream keeps `.glyphs` files as the source of truth and stores a derived UFO copy. Every later edit builds on the source format, so it had to be fixed before the first change.

UFO plus designspace is plain text, diffs cleanly, and is supported by every font editor and by fontmake and fontc without a conversion step. Keeping `.glyphs` files would keep the 435 Glyphs metric keys, but needs a proprietary editor for a clean round trip and ties every build to glyphsLib behaviour. `.glyphspackage` diffs like UFO but is still tied to the Glyphs app.

Undoing this would break the build and throw away every edit made in the UFO sources since the conversion, including the independent Regular master.

## What we gave up

- **Glyphs metric keys.** The 435 `widthMetricsKey` and similar keys do not exist in the UFOs, so opening the sources in the Glyphs app no longer propagates sidebearings from base glyphs. Widths are explicit, so output does not change. A lossless round trip to Glyphs is gone.
- **Automatic upstream merges.** An upstream `.glyphs` change cannot be applied with `git merge`; it has to be repeated by hand as a UFO edit.
- **Bit-identical intermediate weights.** Static instances other than Thin, Regular and Black differ by at most 1 font unit (2000 per em) in some outline points and kerning values compared with interpolating Thin and Black directly, because intermediate weights now interpolate from the Regular master and rounding ties resolve differently. Advance widths differ by at most 1 unit in one weight (ExtraLight Italic).
- **Bit-identical outlines to a Glyphs-app build.** Building through gftools builder instead of fontmake directly from `.glyphs` changes the quadratic approximation of overlapping composites such as `aringacute` and `Aringacute` by under 1.5k square font units of area (below one unit of edge shift). Renderings are identical at display sizes.

## Revisit if

- The project needs to be edited mainly in the Glyphs app.
- Upstream Public Sans moves to UFO as its source of truth, making a scripted merge possible.
